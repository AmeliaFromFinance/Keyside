Stream deck app for your phone

Your phone is the stream deck

Switch scenes, mute mics, start the recording and run Streamer.bot actions from any phone, tablet or browser. It all stays on your own network.

Everything a stream deck needs

A small app on your PC runs the keys. Phones, tablets and Stream Decks show them and stay in sync.

Works on what you have

The Android app, or a browser on iPhone, iPad and computers. Layouts fit portrait, landscape and big screens.

Live keys

See which scene is on air, who is muted and how long you've been live, as it happens.

Stream Decks too

Plug one in and it gets a layout of its own, dials and touch strip included. Elgato plugins install as well.

Smarter keys

Multi-actions with if and otherwise, multi-state keys, long press, double tap, folders, sliders and timers.

Chat triggers

A !command, words in chat or a channel point reward can press a key, with a cooldown.

OBS overlays

Timers and text as browser sources, with your own font, colours, glow and outline.

Moderator access

Give a mod a phone that only sees the pages you pick. A PIN keeps a lost phone out.

Share layouts

As a code or a file. Imported steps that control apps stay off until you approve them.

Try it

These keys behave like the real ones do with OBS. Switch the scene, mute the music, start recording and go live.

  1. Scenes light up when they're on air.
  2. Mutes turn red, so you can see it from across the room.
  3. Record and Go live show how long they've been running.

One minute to set up

No account and nothing to sign up for. A setup guide walks you through it the first time.

  1. Install Keyside on your PC. It sits in the tray and opens the editor.
  2. Pick your apps. You get a ready-made layout with a page for each.
  3. Scan the QR code with your phone and check both screens show the same six digits.
  4. Push to phones and press away. Change any key in the editor whenever you like.

Private by design

No account, no cloud and no telemetry. Your phone talks straight to your PC, encrypted end to end after you pair it with a QR code and a code you compare on both screens.

Plugins run in a sandbox: they can't read your files or start programs, and only reach the addresses you allow. Keyside isn't affiliated with Elgato, OBS or any app it works with.
