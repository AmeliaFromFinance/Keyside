Your phone is the stream deck.

Switch scenes, mute mics, start the recording and run Streamer.bot actions from any phone, tablet or browser. Free, open source, and it all stays on your own network.

Download for your PC Source on GitHub

Windows, macOS and Linux on the PC. Android, iOS or any browser on the deck.

Set up in three steps

  1. Install it on your PC

    Keyside sits in the tray and opens the editor. Nothing to sign up for.

  2. Scan the code with your phone

    Check that both screens show the same six digits, then approve. That device is now paired, encrypted end to end.

  3. Drag actions onto keys

    Push to your phones and press away. Layouts adapt to portrait, landscape, tablets and desktop browsers.

How Stream Deck plugins work

Missing something?

Write a plugin in TypeScript, or let your own tools press keys and change them over the HTTP API.

Writing plugins

Private by design

No account, no cloud

Your phone talks straight to your PC. Nothing is sent to us, and there's no telemetry.

Pairing you can check

Single-use QR codes, a code to compare on both screens, approval on the PC and an optional PIN.

Plugins in a sandbox

Plugins can't read your files or start programs, and only reach the addresses you allow.
